THE PROBLEM

The sky isn't always available.

UAV autonomy leans hard on satellite fixes — but low-altitude flight routinely happens in exactly the places GNSS struggles: under cover, between structures, in terrain shadow, or under deliberate interference.

Urban canyons

Reflective facades and blocked sky views cause multipath and dropped satellite locks.

Indoor & covered areas

Warehouses, tunnels, and hangars remove satellite line-of-sight entirely.

Mountainous terrain

Sky occlusion and steep reflections corrupt satellite ranging accuracy.

Jamming & spoofing

RF interference or hostile spoofing makes a satellite fix actively unreliable.

THE GAP

Low-altitude UAVs need continuous, accurate positioning precisely in the places GNSS is weakest — so the navigation source has to come from the ground, not the sky.

HOW A FIX IS COMPUTED

Ranges in. A locked position out.

Fixed anchors resolve distance to the UAV by time-of-flight. Those ranges are combined with onboard sensing inside an Extended Kalman Filter, which rejects bad readings before they can corrupt the estimate.

UWB anchors time-of-flight ranging
IMU accelerometer + gyroscope
Barometer relative altitude
Magnetometer heading reference
Optical flow ground-relative velocity

EXTENDED KALMAN FILTER

predict → correct → fault-check

Measurement-consistency checks reject outlier ranges before they reach the state estimate.

FUSED STATE ESTIMATE

  • Position — x, y, z
  • Velocity & heading
  • Navigation continuity flag
  • Fault / integrity status

STAYING LOCKED WHEN RF LIES

RF ranging is noisy. The filter assumes that.

Multipath, non-line-of-sight anchors, and interference will happen. The system is built to catch a bad measurement before it degrades the position estimate.

Consistency checks

Every new UWB range is cross-validated against the current state estimate before it's accepted.

Innovation testing

EKF innovation is monitored statistically; measurements outside expected bounds are flagged.

Outlier & NLOS rejection

Anchors reporting inconsistent or non-line-of-sight ranges are down-weighted or excluded.

Graceful degradation

If UWB drops out, IMU, barometer, and optical flow sustain short-term dead reckoning.

VALIDATION PATH

From ground rig to flight test.

The fusion pipeline is proven incrementally — de-risked on a wheeled ground platform long before it flies.
